What we do

From the first prototype to the App Store and Google Play — and every update after.

Privacy and secure connectivity

Networking software that protects users’ internet traffic and personal data with modern, open encryption protocols.

Mobile applications

Apps for iOS and Android: product design, development, testing, release and regular updates.

Cloud infrastructure

Distributed servers, monitoring and automation that keep our services fast and reliable.

Subscriptions and support

Subscription management and customer support built into our apps, so users can change their plan and get help in one place.

How we work

Trust is built into the product from the start.

  1. 01

    Privacy by default

    Our products process only the data they need to work. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

  2. 02

    Security at every layer

    Encrypted connections, least-privilege access to our systems and careful handling of encryption keys are part of our everyday engineering.

  3. 03

    Built to app store standards

    We develop and publish our apps in line with the App Store Review Guidelines and the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

  4. 04

    Open and accountable

    We publish a privacy policy and a support contact for every app we release. Our company details are public and can be checked in the state register.
